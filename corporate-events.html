The questions we get asked on the daily.

If yours isn't here, just send us a message on WhatsApp. We're a small team, and we'd much rather talk it through than have you guess.

— The Kyrah Team
How far in advance should I book?
Honestly, the earlier the better. For big installations we usually need a few weeks to source the right stems. For smaller table arrangements, we can often squeeze you in with a few days' notice if you send us a message.
Do you handle the setup on the day?
We do. We'll arrive early, set up the arrangements, style the tables or entrance, and then come back to collect everything once the event is over. You shouldn't have to worry about a thing.
Can you match our brand colours?
Yes. If you have a Pantone or a specific palette in mind, let us know. We'll source flowers, ribbons, and foliage that sit beautifully with your branding, rather than just guessing.
Do you travel outside of Ajman?
Always. We deliver and install across Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, and the wider UAE. Just let us know the venue when you reach out, and we'll factor the travel into the plan.
How does pricing work?
Because every event is different, we price per project. Tell us the venue, the scale, and the vibe you're going for, and we'll put together a clear quote with no hidden extras.
