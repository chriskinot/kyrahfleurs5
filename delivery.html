Delivery, answered

A few things people ask us most. If yours isn't here, send us a message — we'd rather talk it through than leave you guessing.

Ask us anything
How do the cutoff times work?
Each emirate has a cutoff time based on how far it is from our Ajman studio. Order before the cutoff and we can deliver the same day. Order after and the earliest delivery rolls to the next day. The calendar always shows the earliest available date for the area you pick.
Can I request a specific delivery time?
You can pick a window — morning, afternoon or evening — at checkout. We'll do our best to hit it. If the driver is going to be more than 30 minutes outside the window, we'll message you before they leave the studio.
What happens if I miss the cutoff?
The date picker will automatically move you to the next available day. You can still place the order — it just won't arrive today. If it's urgent, message us on WhatsApp and we'll see what we can do.
Is delivery included in the price?
Ajman delivery is free. Other emirates have a fee that covers the drive. The fee is shown clearly per area when you pick it, and confirmed on WhatsApp before we deliver.
Do you deliver to hotels and offices?
Yes — hotels, offices, villas, apartments, hospitals. If there's a reception desk or a concierge, we'll leave it with them and confirm via WhatsApp. If you need a specific contact person to receive it, add their details in the notes.
